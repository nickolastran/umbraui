## Usage notes

Pass `src` (glTF / GLB) or `model` (React Three Fiber elements).

## Accessibility

- A labelled `group` with a `toolbar` of real buttons: rotate, zoom, reset, pause.
- With focus inside: ←/→/↑/↓ rotate, +/− zoom, Home resets.
- Auto-rotation has a pause button and stops on first interaction (WCAG 2.2.2).
- Under reduced motion it keeps rendering but never moves on its own.
